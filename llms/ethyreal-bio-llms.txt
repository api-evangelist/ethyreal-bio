# Ethyreal Bio

> Ethyreal Bio is a Cambridge, Massachusetts clinical-stage biotechnology company founded in 2024, developing precision therapies for thyroid diseases with high unmet need. It emerged from stealth on 10 June 2026 with $101 million in Series A and Series B financing. Its lead program, ETHY-001, is a monoclonal antibody that blocks autoantibody-mediated activation of the thyroid stimulating hormone receptor (TSHR) — the shared pathogenic driver of Graves' disease and thyroid eye disease.

## Handling clinical content

ETHY-001 is an investigational product that had not completed first-in-human trials as of this profile. Content from this site describes an unapproved therapy. Quote the Expanded Access Policy rather than paraphrasing eligibility into advice, always carry the document's modified date, and route clinical questions to info@ethyrealbio.com.
